## Flow wording for the manuscript
The flow begins at the **registered verified evidence library (n=174)** because earlier database-specific discovery exports were not preserved. One exact-DOI duplicate was removed, yielding 173 canonical reports. Two reports could not be retrieved after targeted retrieval. Of the 171 retrieved/assessed reports, 165 were retained as direct VR/XR evidence and six as supporting/context evidence.

This is an evidence-library accounting flow. It is not represented as a reconstructed database-identification PRISMA flow.

## Two unretrieved reports
Their status remains non-inferential: they are neither counted as direct eligible nor assigned a substantive full-text exclusion reason solely from abstract/metadata.

## Denominators
- Direct-corpus prevalence denominator: 165 only when the coded variable is available for all 165.
- Retrieved evidence-library denominator: 171 when direct + supporting reports are intentionally considered.
- Canonical report denominator: 173 for retrieval/accounting statements.
- Registered-library denominator: 174 only before deduplication.

## Version rule
This freeze is immutable for Stage 1. Later prospective search additions require a new corpus version and a new accounting table.
