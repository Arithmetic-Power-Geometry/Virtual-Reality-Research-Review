# Figure N04 — Failure-aware Vis.-Poly versus S2C comparison

Use two coordinated manuscript panels generated from Figure_N04_controller_geometry_data.csv:
(A) completion rate by controller and geometry;
(B) mean resets/100 m among completed runs.

Do not present Panel B alone because S2C completion is geometry-dependent. G03 is the strongest stress regime: S2C completes 86/100 versus Vis.-Poly 100/100, and among the 86 complete pairs S2C has +0.7193 resets/100 m relative to Vis.-Poly.

Caption boundary: declared R1 benchmark with identical paired virtual paths. Completion/failure and reset burden are separate outcomes. Complete-run reset means are conditional on completion and must not be interpreted as unconditional controller superiority.
