# Master Bibliography Expansion Plan

Current verified BibTeX entries: 21

Target before manuscript freeze: 200+ **valid, relevant, non-duplicate references across the full VR evidence corpus**, not 200 RDW papers and not artificial padding.

## Admission gate
Each entry requires:
1. exact title;
2. complete author list when authoritative metadata exposes it;
3. publication year;
4. venue;
5. volume/issue/pages or article number when applicable;
6. DOI when one exists;
7. authoritative verification source;
8. relevance tag/workstream;
9. duplicate DOI/title check.
