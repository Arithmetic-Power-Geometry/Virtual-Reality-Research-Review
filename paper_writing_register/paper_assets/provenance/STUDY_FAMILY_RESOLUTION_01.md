# Study-Family Resolution — Initial Map

This map prevents systematic-review double counting.

## Rules
- Distinct primary studies remain distinct when they evaluate different algorithms, participants, environments, or datasets.
- Reviews/taxonomies summarize existing evidence and are not counted as new replications of the primary studies they cite.
- Toolkits/software papers support reproducibility and method infrastructure but are not effect-size evidence.
- Foundational measurement instruments are distinguished from later reviews of those instruments.
- Method lineages are grouped for synthesis while preserving individual-study rows.

This is the first family-resolution pass. Additional families will be added during full-text assessment and citation chasing.
