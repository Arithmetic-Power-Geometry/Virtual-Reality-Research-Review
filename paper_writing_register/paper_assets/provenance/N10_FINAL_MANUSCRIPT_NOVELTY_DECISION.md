# N10 Final Manuscript Novelty Decision

## Decision
**N10 is closed for Paper 1 with a negative controller-novelty decision.**

The current paper will **not** claim a novel redirected-walking controller, selector, or adaptive switching algorithm.

## Basis
The repository's prior-art audit establishes occupied territory for:
- context-dependent strategy switching (Adaptive Redirection);
- reinforcement-learning switching among established controllers (SRC);
- future-position-conditioned redirection (F-RDW);
- APF target steering (APF-S2T);
- learned spatial-walkability control;
- predictive reset;
- dynamic geometric mapping and alignment.

The controlled N07/N08 evidence establishes a genuine controller-by-geometry regime effect, and N09 establishes materially different decision costs. These findings motivate a research question but do not by themselves establish algorithmic novelty.

## Candidate retained for future work
A narrower candidate remains scientifically interesting: selection or certification over a joint regime surface containing:
1. failure probability;
2. reset burden conditional on completion;
3. controller decision cost;
4. uncertainty;
5. held-out geometry/path generalization.

This candidate is **not a contribution claimed by the present review paper**. It may be advanced in a separate study only after exhaustive prior-art checking, preregistration, implementation, and held-out evaluation.

## Manuscript consequence
Paper 1 may safely claim:
- a systematic evidence/comparability framework;
- a controlled empirical demonstration of controller-by-geometry regime dependence;
- failure-aware separation of completion and conditional reset burden;
- computational-cost comparison under the common runner;
- reproducibility/provenance lessons from the raw-artifact correction.

Paper 1 must not claim:
- a new RDW controller;
- a new adaptive selector;
- first geometry-aware switching;
- first predictive controller selection;
- C4/C5 algorithmic novelty.

## Status
N10: **CLOSED_FOR_PAPER_1 — NO_ALGORITHM_NOVELTY_CLAIM**.
