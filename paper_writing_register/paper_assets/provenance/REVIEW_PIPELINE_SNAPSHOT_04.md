## N10 implication
The literature clearly occupies:
- context-aware strategy combination;
- dynamic controller selection from physical/virtual environment;
- future-position forecasting;
- APF target steering;
- learned spatial-walkability control;
- predictive reset;
- dynamic geometric mapping.

Therefore a generic geometry-aware/adaptive controller selector cannot satisfy the novelty gate.

The remaining candidate gap is narrower: a reproducible regime model that jointly treats failure probability, conditional reset burden, controller computational cost, uncertainty, and held-out geometry/path generalization.

This snapshot does not imply that all 153 canonical records have been full-text assessed. Only the seven priority records above are marked ELIGIBLE at this stage.
