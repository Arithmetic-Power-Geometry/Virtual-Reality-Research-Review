## Core decision
The paper does not claim a new redirected-walking controller. Existing literature already occupies adaptive, predictive, learned, and strategy-switching territory. The paper instead contributes an operational comparability/reproducibility architecture and a controlled, failure-aware demonstration that controller ordering changes across the tested geometries.

## Validation boundary
No live-user validation is included. Simulation results are not used to infer cybersickness, presence, comfort, preference, perceptual detectability, or usability.
