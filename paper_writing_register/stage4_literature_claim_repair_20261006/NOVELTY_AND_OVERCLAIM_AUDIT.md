# Stage 4 Novelty and Overclaim Audit

## Claims removed
The final paper must not use any of the following:
- "first comprehensive review of virtual reality";
- "first comparability framework for VR";
- "first geometry-aware redirected-walking method";
- "novel RDW controller";
- "novel adaptive controller selector";
- "universal best controller";
- "geometry proves controller X is superior";
- "lower simulated reset burden reduces cybersickness";
- "the measured microseconds establish real-time HMD performance";
- "87.5% of VR contradictions are false";
- "open-source code makes a study reproducible";
- "the review is fully reconstructible from raw database search exports".

## Claims retained after repair
The final paper may state:
1. The study organizes the frozen evidence library through a 21-workstream architecture.
2. It operationalizes semantic comparability using six explicit fields.
3. Seven of eight **pre-specified audited contradiction families** are comparability-limited; the eighth is a controlled RDW regime effect.
4. It uses a six-level operational reproducibility taxonomy.
5. The RDW benchmark separates completion from reset burden conditional on paired completion.
6. All three tested controller pairs change the sign of their conditional reset-burden difference across the four declared geometries.
7. The common-runner controller-decision microbenchmark shows materially different computational overhead.
8. Immutable workflow artifacts corrected stale summaries before confirmatory inference.
9. No new RDW controller/selector is claimed because the adjacent adaptive/predictive design space is already occupied.

## Language discipline
Use **"within the tested benchmark"**, **"among the eight audited families"**, **"operational taxonomy"**, **"common runner"**, **"conditional reset burden"**, and **"registered curated evidence-library flow"** wherever those qualifiers are scientifically material.

Avoid "proves" except for mathematical statements. Prefer "supports", "shows within the benchmark", "is consistent with", or "does not support".

## Live-user boundary
No live-user validation is added. Human-facing constructs from the literature remain contextual evidence only. The RDW experiment itself supports no claim about cybersickness, presence, comfort, detectability, preference, workload, usability, or behavior outside the simulator.
