# G01 Preliminary Cross-Review Findings

These findings are signals for deeper mining, not final novelty claims.

## F01 — Comparison validity
A 2024 systematic review of IVR STEM comparisons reported that only 26% of examined comparisons were fully controlled on five key criteria, while 40% had at least one instructional/content confound. The repository therefore audits comparison validity before treating published head-to-head results as benchmark evidence.

Status: C1 candidate cross-domain methodological gap.

## F02 — Measurement fragmentation
A 2024 scoping review identified 56 VR questionnaires across seven constructs. Measures addressing nominally similar constructs could differ in content, and reliability/validity evidence was not universal. Cross-study synthesis therefore requires construct and instrument compatibility checks.

Status: C2 documented methodological problem; field-wide consequences require primary-study extraction.

## F03 — Scale is not evidence completeness
A 2026 scientometric analysis used 36,450 Web of Science records from 2000–2024. This demonstrates the scale of the field, but bibliometric structure alone cannot determine claim validity, comparison fairness, contradiction, reproducibility, or benchmarkability.

Status: design justification.

## F04 — Reviews are strongly siloed
The verified seed set now separately covers avatars/agents, questionnaires, security/privacy, higher education, visual quality, clinical interventions, haptics, walking-related sickness, social teamwork, accessibility/haptics, AI-driven characters, presence assessment, and open-source experimentation toolkits. The current seed search has not identified a review integrating claim-level evidence, comparison validity, contradictions, reproducibility and benchmarkability across these strata.

Status: C1 candidate contribution; broader closest-review search is required before claiming novelty.

## F05 — TVCG precedent supports artifact/reproducibility analysis
The 2026 TVCG scoping review of 67 open-source VR experimentation toolkits spans design/orchestration, multimodal capture/synchronization, analysis/visualization, and remote/cross-device studies, and recommends interoperable schemas, verified timing, durable exports, and reproducible replayable datasets.

Status: direct methodological precedent and boundary condition for A17.

## F06 — Presence remains methodologically heterogeneous
A 2026 systematic review reports no consensus on a presence-assessment protocol and highlights heterogeneity in instruments, experimental setups, stimuli and physiological approaches.

Status: C2 documented measurement problem; algorithmic opportunity not yet established.
