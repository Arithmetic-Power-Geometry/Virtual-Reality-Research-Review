# Paper Readiness Gate

The paper will be written only after the research artifact is frozen.

Required before drafting:
1. systematic-review evidence extraction and contradiction/gap audit complete;
2. primary technical baselines reproduced or explicitly bounded by reproduction tier;
3. semantically compatible paired comparisons complete;
4. candidate gap reaches at least C4 before motivating a new algorithm;
5. if a new algorithm is justified: implementation, closest baselines, ablations, robustness, uncertainty/statistics and computational cost complete;
6. all figures/tables derive from machine-readable workflow artifacts;
7. references and claims verified;
8. repository workflow green and final artifact hashes frozen.

Drafting then proceeds from evidence to Methods, Results, Discussion and review synthesis; it does not precede the experimental record.
