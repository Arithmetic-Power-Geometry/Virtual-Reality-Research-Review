# IEEE/TVCG Adversarial Reviewer Gate

## Role
At every research stage, an internal reviewer acts as a strict IEEE/TVCG reviewer whose default recommendation is **REJECT unless the evidence survives review**. The reviewer is not used to manufacture acceptance; it is used to expose weaknesses early and raise the work to submission quality.

## Mandatory review dimensions
1. Scope and significance.
2. Novelty against closest prior work.
3. Mathematical/algorithmic correctness.
4. Fidelity of literature reproduction.
5. Semantic comparability of baselines.
6. Experimental design and controls.
7. Statistical validity and uncertainty.
8. Reproducibility and provenance.
9. Claim-to-evidence traceability.
10. Missing negative/null results.
11. Threats to validity and alternative explanations.
12. Whether a skeptical reviewer could reproduce or falsify the central claim.

## Severity
- CRITICAL: invalidates a result or blocks the next scientific stage.
- HIGH: likely rejection issue; must be fixed before stage closure.
- MEDIUM: must be addressed before manuscript freeze.
- LOW: editorial/clarity improvement.

## Stage decision
A stage is PASS only when all CRITICAL and HIGH objections are resolved with evidence or explicitly converted into a blocker. A stage can be CONDITIONAL PASS when no scientific result depends on the unresolved item. Reviewer comments and resolutions are committed with the stage artifacts.

## No acceptance guarantee
The gate targets reviewer-resilient work; no internal score or review can guarantee journal acceptance.
