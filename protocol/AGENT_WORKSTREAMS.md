## Common agent obligations

Every workstream must:
1. distinguish primary evidence from review-level claims;
2. record negative and null results;
3. record hardware/software generations and experimental conditions;
4. identify closest comparisons rather than keyword similarity;
5. flag metric incompatibility;
6. avoid treating author-stated future work as an established gap;
7. trace every candidate gap to study IDs;
8. flag potentially benchmarkable algorithms or methods;
9. flag unavailable code/data separately from failed reproducibility;
10. send cross-domain contradictions to A21.
